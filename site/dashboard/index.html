Conditions above the cutoff, by facility

Every facility and condition

An ERR above the cutoff means more readmissions than CMS expected for that hospital's patients, and the hospital's Medicare payments are cut. The cutoff is 1.0 for FY2013 to FY2018. From FY2019 CMS compares each hospital with the median ERR of its peer group (hospitals with a similar share of patients on both Medicare and Medicaid), published each year in its supplemental file. The only rows where 1.0 stands in for a median CMS did not publish are FY2023 pneumonia, which CMS left out of that year's payment program, and hospitals missing from a year's file; the table marks them.

Contoso's 12 facilities are real public Ohio CMS Certification Numbers (CCNs) relabeled with invented names; no Contoso number describes a real hospital.

These are probabilities, not forecasts of the ERR or the penalty amount. The model scored an AUC of 0.868 on FY2026 but only 0.679 on FY2027, and its FY2027 probabilities are overconfident at both ends (pairs under 0.1 were above the cutoff 21 percent of the time, pairs over 0.9 only 75 percent). FY2028 follows FY2027, so expect accuracy closer to the lower figure. Use the ranking to decide where to look first.

A low follow-up rate beside a condition above the cutoff is a place to look, not evidence that calls lower the ERR: that is the question the follow-up pilot is designed to test. Payer savings, not Contoso's (estimates)

Medicare's and insurers' money. Shown apart and never added to Contoso's net.

Penalty = (1 minus CMS's payment adjustment factor) x estimated base operating DRG payments; CMS publishes the factor but not the payments, so the base is estimated from the CMS IPPS impact file and Table 1B. Contoso's facilities are real public Ohio CCNs under invented names, so the penalty inputs are real CMS figures. Program costs are built from BLS wage data and stated time, setup and management assumptions.

Trials find no effect from a follow-up call alone (Boggan 2025, risk difference 0.00 across 7 trials). The program pays for itself on Contoso's ledger if it lowers the 30-day readmission rate by about half a point ( points at the central cost), which the pilot, with a 3.82-point minimum detectable effect, cannot confirm. The 1.0-point central effect is a judgment; 3.0 points is a multicomponent bundle (Leppin 2014), not a call alone. Payer savings use $14,000 per avoided readmission. Penalty avoided holds FY2027's peer medians, DRG payment ratios and base payments fixed.
