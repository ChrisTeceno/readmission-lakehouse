# PHI note

Date: 2026-10-21. Contoso Health is a fictional client.

## Position

No protected health information (PHI) is used anywhere in this project. Every table is tagged
`contains_phi = false` (`docs/governance.md`, `docs/gold.md`).

| Data | What it is | Why it is not PHI |
|---|---|---|
| CMS HRRP, Hospital General Information, Unplanned Hospital Visits | Facility-level aggregates published by CMS | No patient is identifiable; counts under 25 are suppressed by CMS |
| Census ACS | County-level survey estimates | Public aggregates |
| MTSamples notes | Public transcription samples (2,357 notes) | Published sample reports, not records from a provider's system; used as a stand-in for Contoso's notes (`docs/llm-extraction.md`) |
| Contoso operational database | Facilities, monthly discharge counts and follow-up call counts per facility and condition | Synthetic, generated by `scripts/seed_contoso.py`; counts only, no patient rows (`docs/lakebase.md`) |

Contoso's 12 facilities use real public CMS Certification Numbers relabeled with invented names,
so no real hospital's own data is altered or implied (README).

## What would change with real PHI

1. **Agreements.** A business associate agreement with Databricks, and with the cloud provider
   behind the workspace, before any PHI enters. Free Edition is not suitable; a paid workspace in
   a region and tier covered by the agreement is. Each model endpoint used on PHI must be covered
   too (ADR 0002).
2. **De-identification.** Decide per source whether the lakehouse needs identified data. For
   readmission analytics most of it does not: strip direct identifiers at ingestion (Silver) and
   hold only a pseudonymous key, using Safe Harbor or expert determination for any research use.
   Free-text notes need a de-identification step before extraction, and the extracted evidence
   spans would still be PHI if the notes are not de-identified (ADR 0002).
3. **Unity Catalog controls.**
   - Row filters so a user sees only the facilities they are responsible for.
   - Column masks on identifiers, dates and any free text.
   - Tags such as `contains_phi = true` on tables and columns, with grants only to named
     account-level groups (the analyst grant already written in `docs/governance.md` would apply
     unchanged on a workspace with an account group).
   - Separate schemas for identified and de-identified data.
4. **Audit.** Turn on Databricks audit logs and Unity Catalog system tables for access, retain
   them for the period the agreement requires, and review access to PHI-tagged tables.
5. **No external LLM calls.** Notes would be sent only to Databricks-hosted endpoints inside the
   workspace through `ai_query`, as ADR 0002 does for the public notes, never to an external API.
   Prompts and outputs would be logged only to stores with the same controls as the notes.
6. **Minimum necessary.** Load only the columns a question needs; keep date granularity at month
   or year where day is not needed; drop notes sections that carry no risk factor; set retention
   on Bronze and landing volume files; keep PHI out of MLflow artifacts, logs, error messages
   and dashboard datasets. The extraction schema already asks for a short quote rather than the
   whole note.
7. **Secrets and networking.** Keep credentials in Key Vault or secret scopes (already the
   rule), restrict workspace networking per the client's security review, and keep the
   Lakebase's OAuth-token access (ADR 0001) rather than a stored password.

## What this note does not do

It is a design note, not legal advice. Contoso's privacy officer decides the controls that apply.
